Getting Started: Your Golf Learning Path

You do not need to master everything in one session. Use this short checklist to keep your next practice trip focused.

  1. Start with a comfortable grip, balanced stance, and clear target line.
  2. Learn what each club is designed to do before choosing a shot.
  3. Practice one skill at a time and notice what changes your results.
